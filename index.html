Nº 01 — About

A small start,
a big sky.

I'm Jeffery, and I'm in the first year of B.Tech in Information Technology. A year ago I only used computers; now I'm slowly learning how they actually think, and I find that more exciting than I expected.

I'm the kind of person who looks up at the night sky and wonders what's out there, then looks at a blank code editor and wonders the same thing. Both feel like unexplored space to me.

I'm not an expert yet and I won't pretend to be. What I do have is curiosity, patience, and the habit of finishing what I start. This site is my first proper map. I'll keep redrawing it as I go.
